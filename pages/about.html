Short Autobiography

Hi! I am Cody Wiebe-Kehler. I am an aspiring software developer, and am currently in my first year towards a diploma at Red River College (RRC). Before that I did 2 years at the University of Manitoba in computer science, and took computer programming classes in high school. I've been passionate about tech and have been playing around with computers for as long as I can remember.

Outside of computer science, I like playing video games and watching hockey (GO Jets!). I always enjoy playing some good board games with friends, especially strategy games. I would consider myself a bit of a maker, always with a project or two on the go, and always more in my head!
